Security, stated as refusals

A Business Associate Agreement is signed before anyone is given a login. Every person works under a named individual account in your own system. Nothing is exported, printed or copied to removable media. Access ends the same day a person comes off your account, and when an engagement ends we confirm in writing that we hold nothing of yours.

Most security pages list what a vendor has. Certificates are cheap and a badge proves nothing. This page lists what we will refuse to do, because a refusal is the only part of a security posture that actually costs something to keep.

Named individual logins

One account per person, always. We will not use a shared login even if you offer one, because an audit trail that cannot name a person is not an audit trail.

Nothing leaves the floor

No removable media, no printing, no exports, no personal email. Work happens inside your system and stays there.

BAA before access

A Business Associate Agreement is signed before anyone is given a login. Not after the first week. We will refuse access without it.

Access revoked same day

When somebody comes off your account their access ends that day, and we confirm it in writing.

No data retained

When an engagement ends we confirm in writing that we hold nothing of yours. There is no archive on our side.

Written every Friday

What was done, the numbers, and what went wrong. Including the weeks that went badly, because those are the ones worth reading.

On the floor

  • No phones at the desk
  • No removable media, and USB storage disabled
  • No printing, and no local file storage of client data
  • Personal email and file-sharing sites blocked on the work network
  • Supervised, camera-covered floor
  • Access to a client system limited to the people staffed on that client

On paper

  • Business Associate Agreement signed before first access
  • Confidentiality agreement signed by every person on the floor
  • HIPAA awareness training before a person is staffed on a US healthcare account
  • Access changes confirmed to you in writing the day they happen
  • A named point of contact for any security question, not a ticket queue

The honest limit

No offshore vendor can promise you that a determined person cannot memorise a screen. Anyone who tells you otherwise is selling. What we can promise is that the account is named, the access is minimal, the floor is supervised, and every change is in writing, so that if something did go wrong you would be able to prove who, when and what. That is what an auditor asks for.

Questions

Do you sign a Business Associate Agreement?

Yes, and before anyone is given a login rather than after the first week. If a vendor is willing to start work before the BAA is signed, that tells you what their compliance is worth.

Are you HIPAA certified?

There is no such thing as HIPAA certification. No government body certifies anyone, and any vendor showing you a HIPAA certificate has bought a logo from a training company. What matters is the signed BAA, the technical and administrative safeguards, and whether the controls survive a bad week. Ours are listed on this page and you are welcome to audit them.

Where is our data stored?

In your system, where it already is. We do not copy it, mirror it, warehouse it or process it through anything of ours. There is no Soft Home Global database with your patients in it, which is the only genuinely safe answer to this question.

What stops somebody taking a photograph of the screen?

Phones are not permitted at the desk. The floor is supervised and camera-covered. This is a real risk and no vendor can honestly claim to have eliminated it, so we control it and we do not pretend otherwise.

What happens when a staff member leaves?

Their access to your system ends the same day and we confirm it to you in writing. You can also revoke it yourself at any moment, because the login is yours and was issued by you.

What happens when the engagement ends?

Access is revoked and we confirm in writing that we hold nothing of yours. Since nothing was ever copied out, there is nothing to destroy and nothing to certify the destruction of.
