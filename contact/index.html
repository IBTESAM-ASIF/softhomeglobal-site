Questions before you write

What happens after I send this?

A person reads it and replies the same working day, in plain English. Not an autoresponder and not a sequence. If what you describe is not something a seat fixes, the reply says so.

Will I get put into a sales sequence?

No. One reply from a person. If you do not write back, that is the end of it — you will not receive a drip campaign.

Do I have to get on a call?

No. Most questions can be answered in writing, and every price is published so there is nothing to extract from you on a call. If it is faster to talk, you can pick a time yourself.

What should I not put in the form?

Patient names, dates of birth, member IDs, claim numbers or dates of service. Payers, ageing buckets and dollar amounts are fine and far more useful.
