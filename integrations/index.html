Why no connector

The safest integration is none

Every connector is a copy of your data somewhere else, a credential that outlives the person who created it, and one more thing in a breach notification. A login you issue and can revoke has none of those properties.

Questions

Do you integrate with our system?

No, and that is deliberate. There is no connector, no API key and no sync. A named person logs into your system through an account you issue and works in it exactly as an employee would. Nothing is copied out, so there is nothing to integrate and nothing to breach.

What if our system is not on this list?

The list is what seats have been asked to work in, not a limit. The work is the same in any system and training takes about a week either way. Tell us what you run.

Are you a partner of these vendors?

No. We have no partnership, certification or commercial relationship with any vendor named on this page, and we do not resell their software. They are listed because that is where the work happens.

How do you access our system securely?

A named individual login you issue, never a shared account. Access ends the same day a person comes off your account and we confirm it in writing. You can revoke it yourself at any moment because the credential is yours.
