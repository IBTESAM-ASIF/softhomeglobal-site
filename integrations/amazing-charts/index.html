Practice management and EHR

Amazing Charts billing and AR support

We put trained full-time people inside your own Amazing Charts tenant, through a login you issue, working US hours. No migration, no software to buy, one month minimum.

We are not a partner, reseller or certified vendor of Amazing Charts

Amazing Charts is a product of its own vendor and the name is their trademark. We have built nothing with them, we are endorsed by none of them, and we hold no product certification. What we do is put a trained person into your tenant, under your login, to work your queues. That is the whole claim, and it is checkable on your first call.

What it is

An EHR aimed at small and solo practices, with practice management and billing available alongside it.

Who runs it: Solo providers and very small practices.

How the money moves through it

Billing may run inside the product or through a separate practice management or clearinghouse product depending on how the practice is set up.

The queues a seat works

  • Charge capture and unbilled encounters
  • Rejections
  • Denials
  • Patient balances

What a seat does, day to day

A seat works whichever system produces your claims, starting with a reconciliation of encounters against charges.

What goes wrong here

  • On a solo practice the billing job is usually shared between the provider and one administrator. The first backlog a seat finds is normally work that was started and interrupted.

What this costs

An ar calling & denial management seat is $1,700 per seat per month, full time, in your business hours, inside your own system. Every price we charge is published. One month minimum.

Do you work in Amazing Charts?

Yes — inside your own tenant, through a login you issue. We do not migrate you, we do not require a software change, and we are not a partner, reseller or certified vendor of Amazing Charts. A seat is a trained person working your queues.

What does a seat actually do in Amazing Charts?

What does it cost?

Seats run from $1,500 to $2,500 a month depending on the role, published per role on the pricing page. One month minimum, so the practice risks one month to find out whether it works.
