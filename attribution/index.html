How these were chosen

Images come from Openverse, the WordPress Foundation’s index of openly licensed media, filtered to commercial-use licences and screened to exclude No-Derivatives, since every image here is cropped to a fixed aspect ratio.

Candidates carrying press, ceremonial, military or institutional markers are rejected automatically — those photographs usually contain identifiable people at a named event or another company’s branding, neither of which belongs on this site.
