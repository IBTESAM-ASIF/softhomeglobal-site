# Soft Home Global — how to report a security problem.
#
# Published because every enterprise security review asks for it, and because a company handling
# protected health information under business associate agreements has no business being hard to
# report a vulnerability to. If you have found something, we would rather hear it from you.

# What we ask:
#   Give us a reasonable window to fix it before publishing.
#   Do not access, modify or retain anybody else's data. If you encounter protected health
#   information, stop, and tell us what you saw rather than keeping a copy.
#   Do not run denial-of-service tests or anything that degrades service for the practices
#   who depend on this system during their working day.
#
# What we will do:
#   Acknowledge within one business day.
#   Tell you what we found and when it is fixed.
#   Credit you if you want it and stay quiet if you do not.
