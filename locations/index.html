What changes from state to state

The work is the same everywhere. What changes is the Medicaid programme, the timely filing window, the prevailing wage mechanism and the portal. Each page below carries the fact that is true of that state and not of the others.

California · Medicaid: Medi-Cal

Medi-Cal claims run through a separate submission path from commercial payers, and the timely filing window is shorter than most practices assume.

Texas Medicaid runs through TMHP with its own enrollment and its own claim submission rules, separate from commercial payers.
