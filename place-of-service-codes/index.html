Place of service codes

All 53 codes as CMS publishes them, with the ones that appear on a practice’s claims marked. The two digits in box 24B select the rate more often than the procedure code does, which is why 11 against 22 is a denial and not a typo.

Why place of service changes the money

Why place of service changes the money

A professional service in an office (11) is usually paid at a higher rate than the same service in a facility (19, 22), because in a facility the practice is not carrying the overhead — the facility bills separately for that. Billing 11 for work done in a hospital outpatient department is not a clerical error, it is overpayment.

Telehealth place of service

There are two telehealth codes and the difference is where the PATIENT was, not where the clinician was: 10 when the patient was at home, 02 when they were somewhere else. Payers price these differently.

Telehealth payment policy has changed repeatedly and continues to. Check the payer's current policy and the CMS telehealth list for the date of service before relying on a rate.

Questions

What is the difference between POS 11 and POS 22?

11 is the office: the practice carries the overhead, and the professional service is usually paid at the higher, non-facility rate. 22 is an on-campus outpatient hospital department: the facility bills separately for the overhead, so the professional service is paid at the lower facility rate. Billing 11 for work done in a hospital outpatient department is not a clerical error; it is an overpayment the payer will recover.

What is the difference between POS 19 and POS 22?

Both are hospital outpatient departments. 22 is on the hospital campus; 19 is off campus — a provider-based department at another location. The distinction was created because Medicare pays some off-campus departments differently, and payers use it to apply those rules.

Which telehealth place of service do I use, 02 or 10?

The difference is where the patient was, not where the clinician was. 10 when the patient was at home; 02 when the patient was anywhere else. Payers price the two differently, and telehealth payment policy has changed repeatedly — check the payer’s current policy and the CMS telehealth list for the date of service before relying on a rate.

Does the place of service code change what I am paid?

Yes, and it is the single field on the claim most likely to change the rate without changing the service. The Medicare physician fee schedule has a facility rate and a non-facility rate for most codes, and the place of service code is what selects between them. Commercial payers follow the same shape under their own contracts.

Where does this list come from?

The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services publishes the place of service code set, and it is not licensed. The codes, names and descriptions here are reproduced as CMS prints them, retrieved on 2026-09-11. The notes on which codes change the money are ours.
