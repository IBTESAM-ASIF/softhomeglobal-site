The part the video does not have room for

A seat is one trained person, dedicated to one client, working that client’s queue in that client’s own system during that client’s business hours. Not a shared pool, not a ticket queue, not per-claim piecework.

Seats run from $1,500 to $2,500 a month depending on the role, and every price is published per role. One month minimum — a practice risks one month’s fee to find out whether it works.
